Privacy Policy

Last update: September 6, 2026.

This Policy explains how Western Technology, responsible for Gerenciador PRO (“we”), processes personal data of visitors, users, buyers and partners. Paddle processes data necessary for checkout and transaction according to its own policy.

1. Data processed

  • registration and contact: name, email, telephone/WhatsApp, country, city and preferences;
  • account: authentication identifiers, plan, subscription status and permissions;
  • usage: records entered into the application, preferences, language and interactions;
  • technical: IP address, browser, device, logs, essential cookies, security and diagnostics;
  • purchase: transaction identifier, product, value, currency, status, renewal, cancellation and refund. We do not store complete card details;
  • partnership: source of referral, affiliate identifier, conversion and commission.

2. Purposes and legal bases

We process data to create and protect accounts; provide the contracted service; authenticate users; calculate and maintain access rights; provide support; record consents; process subscriptions with Paddle; assign indications; prevent fraud; comply with legal obligations; and improve performance and security. Depending on the jurisdiction, we use performance of contract, consent, legal obligation and legitimate interests, with respect to the rights of the subject.

3. Sharing

We share only what is necessary with infrastructure operators, authentication, databases, hosting, fulfillment, error analysis, payments and affiliates, including Paddle, Railway, Firebase/Google, Supabase and Affonso when applicable. We may also share for legal obligation, protection of rights or legitimate business reorganization. We do not sell personal data.

4. International transfers

As the service serves users worldwide and uses global suppliers, data may be processed outside your country. We adopt reasonable contracts and safeguards required by applicable laws.

5. Retention

We retain data for the duration of the account and for as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with tax and legal obligations, prevent fraud, and resolve disputes. When possible, data is no longer identifiable or deleted after these deadlines.

6. Security

We apply technical and organizational controls proportionate to the risk, such as HTTPS, access restriction, authentication and security logs. No system is completely immune; relevant incidents will be handled and reported in accordance with the law.

7. Cookies and similar technologies

We use local storage and necessary cookies for session, security, preferences and functionality. Audience measurement or marketing, if applicable, will depend on the legal basis and choices available to the user.

8. Your rights

According to your legislation, you can request confirmation, access, correction, portability, opposition, limitation, review of automated decisions and deletion, in addition to withdrawing consent. You can also complain to the competent data protection authority. To exercise rights, use the Support and indicate “Privacy” in the request.

9. Children

The service is not aimed at minors without legal capacity to contract. If we identify inappropriate processing of minors' data, we will take measures to delete them.

10. Updates

We may update this Policy. Relevant changes will be communicated by appropriate means and the new version will show its effective date.

11. Controller and contact

Controller: Western Technology — Gerenciador PRO. Requests for data must be sent via the Support.
