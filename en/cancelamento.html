Cancellation Policy

Last update: September 6, 2026.

1. Cancellation at any time

You can cancel your subscription renewal at any time. Cancellation normally takes effect at the end of the period already paid; no new charges will be made after that.

2. How to cancel

Open the confirmation email from Paddle and select “Manage subscription”, access the buyer portal at paddle.net, or look for our support channels. We recommend canceling before the renewal date indicated on the portal.

3. Access and data

After requesting cancellation, PRO access remains until the end of the paid period, except for refund, fraud or legal obligation. Afterwards, the account may return to the free tier or have PRO features disabled. Data retention and deletion follow the Privacy Policy.

4. Cancellation and refund

Canceling prevents future charges, but does not automatically generate a refund for the current period. Consult the Refund Policy to find out how to request a return analysis.

5. Price changes

Price changes will be communicated when required and will apply in accordance with the law and the terms presented by Paddle. If you don't accept, you can cancel before the next billing.
